Pokemon Unite

Pokémon UNITE is a free-to-play, 5-on-5 strategic team battle game in the multiplayer online battle arena (MOBA) genre, available on Nintendo Switch, iOS, and Android devices. Players team up to defeat wild Pokémon, level up their chosen Pokémon to evolve, and score points by depositing energy into the opponent's goal zones before the 10-minute timer runs out. The team with the highest score at the end wins.
